# Social Plan / Content Calendar Template

Preserve the user's existing columns when revising a calendar. Adapt this starting
shape to the requested output rather than forcing a new layout. Above the table,
retain the agreed date range/time zone, cadence/week boundaries, audience priorities,
voice/CTA, exclusions and production constraints. Do not invent missing quotas.

| date | status | audience / viewer job | pillar / topic | hook promise / useful payoff | format / duration or slides | supporting evidence / facts | owner | available assets / capture needs | linked brief / version | tracking scope or gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep candidate ideas separate from scheduled posts. Mark a post published only
with evidence. At handoff, identify the current version, unresolved facts/assets,
stale copies, validation performed and the agreed measurement/review point.
